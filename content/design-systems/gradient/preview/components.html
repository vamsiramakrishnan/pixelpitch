Features

Design Principles

Six core principles shape the Gradient system: smoothness, playfulness, depth, consistency, accessibility, and responsiveness.

01

Smooth Transitions

All interactions use eased timing curves. Motion feels natural, never jarring or abrupt.

02

Color Gradients

135° angled gradients add depth without overwhelming. Semantic colors maintain clarity across all states.

03

Soft Shadows

Carefully calibrated elevation shadows create visual hierarchy. Shadows lift on hover to indicate interactivity.

04

Responsive Grid

8pt baseline ensures every component aligns to rhythm. Consistent spacing scales from mobile to desktop.

05

Accessibility First

Sufficient contrast on all gradients. Focus states visible and clear. Inclusive by design, not retrofit.

06

Playful Energy

Modern, youthful personality without sacrifice to professionalism. Every component feels fresh and engaging.

Philosophy

Gradients aren't decoration. They're depth. They're motion. They're the language of modern interfaces that feel alive.
