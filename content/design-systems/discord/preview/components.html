Message Group Layout

Raid Lead Today at 9:42 PM
alright team, tanks up front, dps spread to the sides. Heals stick with the MT.
Raid Lead Today at 9:43 PM
We're going live in 30. Get your keybinds ready. 💪
Warrior Main Today at 9:44 PM
Ready. Shield is full. 🛡️

Messages stack tightly (4–8px between groups). Compact layout means thousands of lines of scrollback remain scannable. Usernames in bold, timestamps muted. Avatars are 40×40px with 8px radius.
