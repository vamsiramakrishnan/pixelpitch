Enterprise Vocabulary

The Cohere component system is built on enterprise-grade patterns: transparent buttons that activate on hover, generously rounded cards (22px), and a chromatic restraint that lets content and interaction shine through.

Buttons

Button States

Cohere's button system prioritizes clarity and confidence. Transparent (ghost) buttons are the default—text shifts to blue on hover. Dark solid buttons anchor call-to-action surfaces. Outlined variants provide secondary affordance.

Card Components

Spacing & Radius Scale

The Cohere spacing scale is base-8. The signature card radius is 22px (--s-8). Buttons use 8px. Smaller elements step down through 4px and 0px.
