Bold Design Manifesto

Lists

Numbered List

Two-column grid layout with optional signal-orange number accent. Use the third or fourth item to highlight a key capability.

1

Heavy Typography

900-weight display faces command attention. Every headline demands visual presence at 48pt and above.

2

High Contrast

Ink on paper. Signal blue for accents. No ambiguity. Every element earns its visual real estate.

3

Direct Interaction

Buttons respond with transform and color inversion. 2px borders and 8px radius. Feedback is immediate and visual.

4

Commanding Layouts

Grid-based composition with generous whitespace. Vertical rhythm guides the eye. Every margin intentional.

5

Predictable Spacing

4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96px scale. No ad-hoc offsets. Alignment compounds across sections and modules.

6

Semantic Color

Signal blue for primary actions. Bone for secondary. Slate for muted text. Green/Red for success/danger states.
