A QUIET REVOLUTION
IN PRESENTATION

F or three decades, software treated slides as flat, lossy artifacts — captured screenshots of design intent, never the intent itself. Slidify is a deliberate departure: every shape, gradient, shadow, and glyph survives the export. What looked like a screenshot is now a first-class object. The deck you compile in the morning becomes the deck a colleague edits in the afternoon, and the difference between raster and editable is no longer a tax you pay at the door.
