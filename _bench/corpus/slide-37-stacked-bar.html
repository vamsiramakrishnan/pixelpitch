Three takeaways

01
Platform share rises ~11 ppts per tier of buyer scale.
Mix shifts ~33 ppts end-to-end — from 28% in SMB to 61% in enterprise — tracking IT-team self-sufficiency more closely than absolute spend.
02
SMB services line is structurally sticky.
Implementation plus managed services hold a combined 53% of SMB wallets, and have moved less than 200 bps in three years.3
03
Training is the swing line for upsell.
Mid-market buyers willing to fund 10–11% of wallet on enablement pay 1.4× the platform multiple of peers, on average.
