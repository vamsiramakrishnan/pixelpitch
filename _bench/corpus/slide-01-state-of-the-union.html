The State of the Union · Economy

Wages climbed, then prices climbed faster

Real median household income outran inflation for most of two decades. After 2021 the lines crossed, and households felt it before the data confirmed it.

By the Editorial Graphics Desk · Updated April 2026
Median wage, indexed (1994 = 100) CPI, all items (1994 = 100)
Inflection points
2001 · Dot-com bust. Wages stall for 18 months while CPI keeps a 2.4% trend.
2008 · Financial crisis. First period since 1994 where real wages decline two years running.
2015 · Recovery gap. CPI lulls below 1.5%; wage index reopens its lead.
2021 · The crossover. Pandemic shock, supply chains, energy spike — CPI overtakes wages.
2024 · Now. Wage index 192; CPI 198. The 6-point gap is what households call "still behind."
Source · BLS CPI-U series and Census ASEC, 1994–2024 · The Times analysis
