What we believe

Five quiet convictions.

We wrote them down so we'd remember on the days the noise got loud. They aren't rules. They're the shape of the work, when the work goes well.

i.

Slow is a feature.

Speed without consideration is just hurry, and hurry leaves a trail.

ii.

Whitespace is a kindness.

What we leave out tells the reader we trust them.

iii.

Make it by hand, when you can.

The wobble is the warmth. We do not optimize the wobble away.

iv.

Soft is not weak.

A round corner has held more weight than a sharp one, in our experience.

v.

Begin where the light is.

If a thing won't start, move closer to a window. Then try again.
