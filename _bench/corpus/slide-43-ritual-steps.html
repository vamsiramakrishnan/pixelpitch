A small daily ritual

Four steps, none of them rushed.

i.
Arrive

Sit before you stand.

Two minutes by the window. No phone, no agenda. Notice the temperature of the room.

ii.
Warm

Make something hot.

Tea, coffee, broth — the choice matters less than the slowness of preparing it.

iii.
Note

One line, by hand.

Whatever is loudest in your chest. Five words is enough; no one is grading.

iv.
Begin

Choose one thing.

Not the urgent thing. The kind one.

Practice, not performance
