Beacon & Lowe — Enterprise Software Practice Section I · Forces Shaping Demand
Exhibit 4 · Five Demand Drivers

Five forces are reshaping enterprise software demand through 2028 — only two are reversible1

Three are structural shifts in buyer behavior; two are policy- or rate-driven and may unwind. Where forces stack, planning windows compress to under nine months.

01Force

Procurement is consolidating — six vendors absorb the budget that twenty-two used to share.

Concentration ratios rose from 41% to 68% of category spend in three years across 412 surveyed buyers; long-tail vendors retain niche use-cases but lose framework agreements first.2

Structural · irreversible by 2028
68%
Top-6 share of category spend, FY 2025; up from 41% in FY 2022.
↑ 27 ppts vs. FY 2022
02Force

Generative-AI features have moved from differentiator to table stakes inside fourteen months.

By Q1 2026, 79% of RFPs included native AI-feature requirements as gating criteria, up from 12% in Q3 2024; vendors without a credible roadmap drop out at first-pass screening.

Structural · accelerating
79%
RFPs gating on native AI features, Q1 2026.
↑ 67 ppts in 18 months
03Force

Capital cost has reset deal economics — payback windows under 18 months are now mandatory.

The median budget-committee threshold tightened from 24 months in 2022 to 17 months in early 2026, reflecting a 280-bps rise in internal hurdle rates; rate cuts could partially unwind this.

Cyclical · partially reversible
17mo
Median payback threshold, Q1 2026; down from 24 months three years earlier.
↓ 7 months vs. 2022
04Force

Data-residency mandates are fragmenting deployment plans across at least three regions.

Sixty-one percent of surveyed buyers now require in-region data planes for at least one workload, against a backdrop of nine major regulatory updates since FY 2023.3

Policy · reversibility low
61%
Buyers requiring in-region data planes for at least one workload.
↑ 19 ppts vs. FY 2023
05Force

Talent gravity has shifted — engineering hubs outside the US now win 38% of senior hires.

Four cities — Bengaluru, Warsaw, Lisbon and Mexico City — absorb the bulk of the shift, materially changing where vendor R&D capacity sits over the planning horizon.

Structural · persistent
38%
Senior R&D hires placed outside the US, FY 2025.
↑ 14 ppts vs. FY 2022
Source: Beacon & Lowe Demand Drivers Index, March 2026; n=412 enterprise software buyers; reversibility classification per the firm's three-criterion rubric (policy, capital, behavior). 1 Forces 03 and 04 classified as partially reversible. 2 Concentration ratio measures top-6 share of cumulative category spend. 3 Counts EU, UK, IN, BR, KR, JP, AU, CA, ZA major updates since FY 2023.
