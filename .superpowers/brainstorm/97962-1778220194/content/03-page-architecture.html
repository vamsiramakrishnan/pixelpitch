Design Philosophy

The page alternates between warm parchment and ink-black contrast sections to create rhythm. Coral (#ed6f5c) is used sparingly — only on numerals, CTAs, and the hero accent word. No decorative blobs, gradients, or AI slop. Every element earns its pixel. Typography does the heavy lifting: Playfair Display for headlines, Inter Tight for UI, JetBrains Mono for code/metadata. Motion is Framer springs only — snappy entrances on scroll, no spectacle.
