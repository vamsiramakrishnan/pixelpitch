What should the method stages be called?

The loop that tells users how PixelPitch works. Each stage gets a section on the landing page.

A

The Studio Method

I. Choose
→
II. Brief
→
III. Stream
→
IV. Refine
→
V. Ship

Literal and clear. Each verb maps 1:1 to a product action. Easy to understand cold.

B

The Craft Loop

I. Discover
→
II. Direct
→
III. Compose
→
IV. Deliver

Higher-level, closer to OD's vocabulary. "Compose" captures both the AI streaming and the human refinement. 4 stages feels tighter.

C

The Design Intelligence Loop

I. Taste
→
II. Craft
→
III. Polish
→
IV. Ship

Abstract and opinionated. "Taste" = choosing your design system + skill. "Craft" = the AI streaming. "Polish" = tweaks. Uses PixelPitch's own vocabulary (craft, taste are core concepts in the codebase).
